Welcome to the 2019 CITL Season!

Week 15 scores have been posted and the 2019 season comes to a close! Congratulations to First Place: Sights Impaired! The last few weeks had been a battle for first, with Second Place: Missed Again nearly taking the lead on a few occasions.

Captains of these teams, please let me know who from your team would like to purchase a trophy. In addition to the standard first and second place trophies, we also have awards for Highest Average, Rookie of the Year, and Most Improved!

Trophies will again be Walnut Birds with Stands from Shooting Sports Souvenirs. The total price per trophy with stand is $21.00. These are high quality walnut birds that we've used for many years! Captains, please coordinate with me on who is interested in purchasing and I will help coordinate a bulk order.
