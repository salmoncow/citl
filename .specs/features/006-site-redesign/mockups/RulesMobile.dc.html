League rules

  1. Returning shooters start with last year's average; new shooters at 35. It drops out after two nights.
  2. Top team each week earns 30 points, then 28, 26… Ties split.
  3. Beat the team's going-in average: +5 bonus.
  4. +1 per qualifying rookie (avg under 35), max 2. None after Week 10.
  5. Season ties on points go to more total targets.
  6. Yardage: total the 5 shooters' averages and look it up below.
