League rules

Agreed at the start of each season by the League Coordinator and team captains. Anything not covered here is decided by a majority of captains.

1General

  1. Rules are agreed at the beginning of each season by the League Coordinator and team captains before being published.
  2. The last team to shoot on a night sends that night's score sheets to the League Coordinator.

2Team structure

  1. A team has between five and fifteen shooters.
  2. Anyone is an eligible shooter. Under 16 requires a parent or guardian present.
  3. A shooter may be added to a team at any time.
  4. Teams may shoot with up to 2 dummies to field a squad.

3League schedule

  1. Regular time and venue: Tuesday evenings at Darnall's Gun Works & Ranges.
  2. When the 4th of July falls on a weekday, the league does not shoot that week.
  3. A team may choose another night or time, but must notify the coordinator before the league begins and keep that slot all season.
  4. We shoot in wind and rain, but not lightning.
  5. If a team shoots and weather then stops the others, that team may reshoot (at its expense) or skip the following week.
  6. A night can be postponed by majority vote of captains present; the schedule extends by a week.
  7. A shooter who starts but can't finish a round must finish before their team shoots the following week. Illness or other reasons: contact the coordinator.
  8. Squad sequence numbers or signup times set shooting order. Teams all present and paid get a signature on their score sheet.

4Makeup rounds

  1. Captains notify the coordinator by email at least 24 hours before shooting outside the regular time.
  2. A team may not fall more than two weeks behind. A missed week not made up by the close of the second week after it stays at zero.
  3. Missed weeks are made up in order. Two rounds in one week: the first counts as the missing week.

At the end of the season the grace period is one week.

5Scoring & yardage

  1. Returning shooters start with last year's average; new shooters start at 35. After two nights the starting average drops out.
  2. Members who haven't shot for 2 years are considered rookies.
  3. Each week the top team earns 30 points, the next 28, and so on. Ties split the points.
  4. Season ties on points go to the team with more total targets.
  5. A team scoring above its going-in average earns 5 bonus points.
  6. 1 bonus point per qualifying rookie shooting that week, max 2. A rookie qualifies with a going-in average under 35. No rookie points after Week 10.
  7. Yardage: total the going-in averages of the 5 shooters for the night and find the starting yardage below.

6Trophies

Trophies go to the 1st and 2nd place teams; members may fund their own and coordinate with the League Coordinator. Individual awards are participant-funded. Shooters must shoot at least 6 nights to be eligible.
