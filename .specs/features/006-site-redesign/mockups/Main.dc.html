NEW TO TRAP?

Your first Tuesday

  1. 1Email the League Coordinator at lmckenna.citl@gmail.com as an individual or a pre-formed team. Enrollment is free.
  2. 2Bring your gear: 12-gauge (or smaller), shells, eye and ear protection.
  3. 3Pay Darnall's range fee and shoot two bunkers — 50 targets.
