Welcome to the 2020 CITL Season!

COVID-19 UPDATE
CITL will delay the start of our 2020 season until Tuesday, May 5th. Updates will be provided as we get closer to that date.

Announcements

The 2020 Central Illinois Trap League will be kicking off this spring on Tuesday, May 5th. The season will go for 13 weeks (adjusted for the COVID-19 delay), ending Tuesday, July 28th. Because 4th of July does not land on a weekday this year there will be no bye week.

Shooters or teams can enroll individually or with a team using the enrollment form. The form is a Microsoft Word .docx (I'm not so fancy that I have an online form set up...). Please have your form submitted no later than Friday, March 20th.

A practice day will be held Tuesday, April 14th to anyone who wants to join! This is a great opportunity for new shooters to learn about the league and pick up on a few techniques from others.

For more information about league fees and location check out the About page.

Central Illinois Trap League is an independently organized, non-discriminating shooting sport. We encourage families and friends to participate. We remind everyone to keep it legal by shooting with an adult if under age, or otherwise having a valid FOID card.
