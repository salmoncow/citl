and are not referenced by the constitution, agents, or skills. They were retired in the July
2026 deep-review cleanup (workstream WS-2):

  in `.specs/constitution.md` §V.2.
- **prompt-maintenance.md** — a 358-line enterprise maintenance process for a prompt library
  that no longer exists. Replaced by the quarterly checklist in `.specs/constitution.md` §VIII.1.
- **speckit-integration-guide.md** — documented an adoption/metrics governance regime that never
  materialized (and had accumulated duplicated/garbled sections). The still-useful "what belongs
  where" guidance lives in the constitution §VII, `.specs/README.md`, and
  `spec-authoring-guidelines.md`.
