Hello, everyone! The 2021 season has officially come to a close. We had a lot of high scores this year with multiple straight 25's and even two straight 50's! I appreciate everyone participating this year, and look forward to kicking off the season again in early 2022. In the meantime, if you have any suggestions as far as the website, rule proposals, or general feedback, reach out to me at tyler.citl@gmail.com.

Trophies will again be Walnut Birds with Stands from Shooting Sports Souvenirs. The total price per trophy with stand is $21.00. These are high quality walnut birds! Captains of teams in the trophy list below, please coordinate with me on who from your team is interested in purchasing and I will put in a bulk order.

2021 Season Kickoff

Updated: 3/20/2021

NEW for 2021 The captains have voted majority in favor of updating the calculations for handicap yardage each week. The calculations can be found in Rules Section 5.6.

To streamline the calculations between bunkers, please use the Yardage Calculator (bottom right-hand corner on this page) to quickly calculate your yardage.

The 2021 league will officially kick off on Tuesday, April 13th. We will align to any state regulations related to COVID and gathering sizes based on the classification of Darnall's business.

To sign up, send an email to tyler.citl@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Team Name
  • Captain's name, email, and phone number
  • Team member names and email
If signing up as an individual, send your name and email and we will work with existing teams for placement.

For more information about league fees and location check the About page.

Schedule

A practice day will be held the Tuesday before the league starts (4/6/2021). This is a great time to meet up with other shooters and work off some cobwebs from last season.

Yardage Calculator

Bunker 1: All shooters start at 16 yards.   Bunker 2: Calculator determines yardage.

 

Central Illinois Trap League is an independently organized, non-discriminating shooting sport. We encourage families and friends to participate. We remind everyone to keep it legal by shooting with an adult if under age, or otherwise having a valid FOID card.
