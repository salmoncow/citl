2022 Season Kickoff

Updated: 1/27/2022

The 2022 league will officially kick off on Tuesday, April 12th. Similar to last year, we will align to any state regulations related to COVID and gathering sizes based on the classification of Darnall's business.

To sign up, send an email to tyler.citl@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Team Name
  • Captain's name, email, and phone number
  • Team member names and email
If signing up as an individual, send your name and email and we will work with existing teams for placement.

For more information about league fees and location check the About page.

Schedule

An informal practice day will be held the Tuesday before the league starts (4/5/2022). This is a great time to meet up with other shooters and work off some cobwebs from last season.
